## Features
- **Timers**: Track your solve times and improve your speed.
- **Analytics**: Gain insights into your performance and progress.
- **Community**: Connect with other cubers and share tips.
- **Resources**: Access tutorials, algorithms, and guides to enhance your skills.
- **Competitions**: Participate in online competitions and challenges.
- **WCA Integration**: Sync your results with the World Cube Association database.
- **Algorithm Trainer**: Learn and practice new algorithms.
- **Scramble Generator**: Generate scrambles for various cube types.
- **Session Management**: Save and review your practice sessions.
- **Leaderboards**: Compete for the top spots in various categories.
- **Multi-Cube Support**: Work with different cube types (2x2, 3x3, 4x4, etc.).
- **Tutorials**: Step-by-step guides for beginners and advanced cubers.
- **Virtual Cubes**: Practice solving with a 3D virtual cube.
- **Algorithm Database**: Access a comprehensive list of algorithms for different methods.
- **Practice Modes**: Various modes to help you practice specific skills.
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your improvement over time.
- **Notifications**: Stay updated with the latest news and events in the cubing community.
- **Social Sharing**: Share your achievements on social media platforms.
- **Daily Challenges**: Engage in daily puzzles and challenges to keep your skills sharp.
- **User Profiles**: Manage your cubing profile.
- **Customization**: Personalize your experience with themes and settings.
- **Mobile Friendly**: Use CubeDev on any device, anywhere.
- **Data Export**: Export your solve data for further analysis.
